FR-362-AC-10 residual, confirmed honest: `scalar_bit_limits_admit_exactly_and_deny_first_excess` keeps only `TC-910`. Source shows `rational-arithmetic.normalize` charges `max(B(num), B(den))` of the unreduced parts, which is always at most the preceding `rational-arithmetic.arithmetic` amount. So neither the high-water `integer_bits` nor a one-below limit can ever expose it through the public `Meter`, and the AC's last sentence is unobservable today. This matches SR-2260 FND-001. The seam belongs to IR-667 (Backlog), and this review does not ask PR #9 to supply it. The rest of AC-10 is asserted (exact limit completes, limit-1 stops with prior consumption, request, prefix and zero results), but the criterion correctly stays untagged and incomplete.
