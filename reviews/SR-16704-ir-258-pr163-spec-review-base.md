## Verdict

FAIL as written (not mergeable yet): 1 high, 4 medium, 5 low. Mergeable once FND-001 to FND-005 are fixed; the lows are one-line edits and should ride in the same round.

What holds: every charge point, order, limit kind and amount in Behaviors 11 and 12 equals the owner. Operands use `max_part_bits` of both operands with `value_occurrences` 2; arithmetic is `max(N, D)` with the `+ 1` on add and subtract only; normalize is `max(magnitude_bits)` of the unreduced numerator and denominator, taken before `Rational::new` reduces them, so the claim that normalize uses unreduced parts is exact; result-retain consumes one result unit; ordering has operands, then `max(B(a)+B(q), B(c)+B(p))` with no `+ 1`, then result-retain. The AC-7 literals check out: 1/2 + 1/3 gives 2, 4, 3; 1/2 + 1/2 normalizes 4/4 at 3. The refusal order in Behavior 3 matches FR-398 as coded on PR #162: zero denominator, then a negative denominator, zero over a denominator other than 1, and the coprime test. The i64 claim is correct. With all parts inside i64, the largest cross product is 2^63 x (2^63 - 1) = 2^126 - 2^63, and a sum of two such products is at most 2^127 - 2^64. An i64::MIN squared is 2^126, and p q < 2^126. Every formed integer therefore fits i128, the cross-product ordering is exact, and reduction never grows a part. CG FR-046 on origin/main needs only addition and equality (its Description reads "exact rational addition and rational equality"). Each of AC-1 to AC-11 has a TC-956 step, AC-11 is PLANNED, and the evidence record is empty. The PR adds no vendoring, SHA, pin or digest.

What fails: TC-956 verifies AC-10 by scanning source text. The `Negation` capacity cause refuses subtractions whose results are representable. The FR-398 helper FR-408 reuses exists only on unmerged PR #162 and is private there. The tractability of the 256-step gcd and the u128 division under Kani is unmeasured, while FR-403-AC-15 is evidence against it. FR-408 changes CG FR-046's contract but says it edits no CG specification.
