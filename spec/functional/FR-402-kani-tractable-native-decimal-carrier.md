# FR-402: A Kani-tractable native Decimal carrier built from the owner's Decimal bindings

## Description

The kernel SHALL provide a native Decimal carrier, a native Decimal type and a constructor that builds a carried value of that type from the two `i64` physical bindings of a Decimal operand, a coefficient then a scale (Behavior 1 to 7). The constructor SHALL use only fixed-width native arithmetic and no allocation, so that a generated Kani proof can draw the bindings symbolically and reach a retained Decimal without arbitrary-precision work.

The consumer is the decimal row of Contract CodeGen (CG FR-051, prerequisite P1, ticket IR-1444). That row draws each operand as the owner's retained representation, a coefficient and a scale constrained by `DecimalType::contains` (CG FR-025-AC-15) and compares the generated oracle with a native expression over the operand. This requirement is the operand and its construction only; it specifies no operation over the carrier. The constructor, like [FR-384](./FR-384-admitted-native-integer-refinement.md)'s native carrier and [FR-398](./FR-398-kani-tractable-carried-quantity-constructor.md)'s constructor, SHALL be available in ordinary host builds, with one source for a host build and a `cfg(kani)` build, so that a host test of it is a test of what Kani proves. It is not a verification-only replacement. The owner's `Decimal`, `DecimalType`, `DecimalRepresentation` and their membership ([FR-372](./FR-372-fallible-bounded-decimal-membership.md), QSpec FR-140) are unchanged.

**Relation to FR-398.** FR-398 maps a Decimal binding to the canonical fraction of a quantity magnitude and does not retain the representation. This carrier retains the representation: the owner's results and charges depend on the retained coefficient and scale, so `1.0` and `1` are different inputs (CG FR-051). The two constructors share the owner membership rule and the same boundary vocabulary, and neither introduces a second membership rule. FR-398 does not depend on this requirement.

Four things are out of scope. Operations over the carrier (arithmetic, rounding and ordering, with their charges) are not specified here, and CG FR-051 cannot be credited with a native expression until they exist. Decoding retained counterexample playback into bindings is CG's (CG FR-051, FR-025-AC-15). A Decimal whose coefficient does not fit a signed 128-bit integer is not carried: it is not reachable from `i64` bindings, and a type whose bound does not fit is refused at ingress (Behavior 2). Choosing a smaller domain so a proof fits is not done here. If a composed proof does not fit, the fallback is a finite-grid bound filed as its own ticket by the owner of IR-1444.

## Use case

A generated harness for `a + b` over `Decimal[0,100; 0,2]` calls `kani::any::<i64>()` twice per operand, the coefficient then the scale. The pair `(5000, 2)` has a raw coefficient outside `[0, 100]` and is the owner's own form of the value `50`. The constructor yields the carried Decimal `(5000, 2)`, retained as drawn, which a native expression takes as an operand.

## Behavior

### Types

1. The native Decimal type SHALL hold two signed 128-bit inclusive coefficient bounds, a minimum and a maximum membership scale (`u32`) and the owner's `RoundingMode`, and SHALL be `Copy` with no drop glue. Its checked constructor SHALL take `(lower: i128, upper: i128, min_scale: u64, max_scale: u64, rounding)` and SHALL return the owner's `IllTyped` with cause `MalformedDecimalType` exactly when the owner's `DecimalType::new` would for the same values (`lower > upper`, `min_scale > max_scale` or `max_scale > u32::MAX`).
2. The native Decimal type SHALL be convertible from an owner `DecimalType` by an ingress function. If a bound of the owner type lies outside signed 128 bits, then the ingress SHALL return `TypeNotRepresentable` and no native type. The owner `DecimalType` is well formed, so the ingress has no other refusal. The ingress is an explicit conversion from general integers outside the proof path and MAY allocate.

### Carrier and construction

3. The carried Decimal SHALL hold the retained representation only: a signed 128-bit coefficient and a `u32` scale. It SHALL be `Copy`, SHALL have no drop glue and SHALL carry no `DecimalType`, `Integer`, `Decimal` or cached normalized form. Two carried Decimals that are numerically equal with different retained scales SHALL be distinct values. Its structural equality SHALL be equality of the retained pair. A total constructor from any signed 128-bit coefficient and any `u32` scale SHALL exist and SHALL grant no membership: the carried Decimal is a retained representation, and membership of a type is decided by the checked constructor of Behavior 4 or by a later operation, as `NativeInteger` construction from an `i128` grants no operation capability in FR-384.
4. The constructor SHALL take a reference to the native Decimal type, the `i64` coefficient and the `i64` scale, in that order, and SHALL return the carried Decimal retaining exactly the pair drawn: no normalization, scaling or reordering. It SHALL perform no allocation, SHALL create no `Integer`, `Decimal` or `Rational`, SHALL contain no loop and no iterator adapter (IR-1436 D4), SHALL take no `Meter`, SHALL charge nothing and SHALL NOT select its body by `cfg(kani)`.
5. The constructor SHALL admit a binding only when it is a member of the type by the owner's rule. If the scale is outside `0..=4294967295`, then the constructor SHALL return `OutsideCarrierDomain`. Otherwise let `(c', s')` be the normalized pair of the binding (trailing decimal zeros removed while the scale is above zero; zero is `(0, 0)`), let `s*` be `max(s', min_scale)`; the binding is a member exactly when `s*` is at most `max_scale` and `lower <= c' x 10^(s* - s') <= upper`. If the binding is not a member, then the constructor SHALL return `OutsideCarrierDomain`. A lifted coefficient whose magnitude exceeds the native width SHALL be compared by sign alone (it exceeds `upper` when positive and falls below `lower` when negative, since both bounds fit the native width) and SHALL never be materialized, wrapped or saturated. The scale range check SHALL precede the membership check, so that no narrowed scale reaches the membership rule.
6. The refusal vocabulary is that of FR-398 reduced to what this carrier can produce. The causes are `TypeNotRepresentable` (at ingress, before any binding exists) and `OutsideCarrierDomain` (at construction), and no other refusal SHALL exist. FR-398's `CarrierMismatch`, `ZeroDenominator` and `NonCanonical` have no counterpart because there is one carrier and the pair needs no canonical form. FR-398's `NotRepresentable` has no counterpart because the carrier retains the pair and never forms the canonical fraction `c / 10^s`: an `i64` coefficient always fits the native coefficient. The check order is: ingress representability of the type, then the scale range, then membership. Each refusal SHALL carry no carried Decimal.
7. The carried Decimal SHALL provide a normalized view returning the pair `(c', s')` of Behavior 5 as a signed 128-bit coefficient and a `u32` scale, with no loop, no allocation and no `Integer`, and SHALL provide a read-out that yields the owner `Decimal` whose `representation()` equals `DecimalRepresentation::new(Integer::from(coefficient), scale)`. The owner `Decimal` computes its own normalized form, which SHALL equal the native normalized view. The read-out may allocate, and its allocation is outside the constructor's zero-allocation window.

### Evidence

8. The kernel SHALL carry a host differential. For every boundary binding of TC-950 whose scale is in `0..=4294967295`, the oracle SHALL be the shipped owner route: `Decimal::new(Integer::from(coefficient), scale)` and `DecimalType::contains` on the owner type, with the owner's `normalized()` for the normalized view. The owner route runs for every such scale, because `contains` never materializes the lifted coefficient and `Decimal::new` forms no power of ten. The constructor and the oracle SHALL agree on admission and on the retained and normalized pairs with exactly one intended difference: a type with a bound outside the native width has no native type, and its ingress refusal `TypeNotRepresentable` is reported where the owner type exists and admits bindings. A binding whose scale is outside `0..=4294967295` has no owner-route result (`Decimal::new` takes a `u32`); it is checked only against its literal `OutsideCarrierDomain` and sits outside the differential.
9. The kernel SHALL carry a Kani proof, without a loop unwind bound, that over all symbolic `i64` bindings and all symbolic native type bounds (`lower <= upper` signed 128-bit, `min_scale <= max_scale` over `u32`, including values at which the lift of Behavior 5 wraps or exceeds the native width) the constructor's outcome and retained pair equal an independent bounded mathematical reference that shares no code with the constructor, with covers that the member path, the out-of-scale-range path and the non-member in-range path are each reachable. A second proof SHALL verify, over all signed 128-bit coefficients and all `u32` scales, that the normalized view satisfies the defining postcondition of Behavior 5 (the pair is the least-scale pair equal in value, never raises the scale, and leaves a coefficient not divisible by ten unless the scale is zero) against a reference that shares no code with it. A host test SHALL bind each reference to the owner route over the boundary set.
10. When CG's decimal row (CG FR-051, ticket IR-259) consumes the delivered carrier, the memory and time of the proof harness that draws its operands through the carrier under the 16 GiB per-request ceiling of CG FR-028 SHALL be measured and recorded under IR-1444 with the peak provenance and the terminal outcome. This requirement states no memory budget and claims no fit: a configured ceiling or a prediction is not a measurement, and the label "K-DR budget" in the text of IR-1444 names no figure in this repository or in CG's specification.

## Status

Planned (IR-1444). No implementation, proof result or measurement exists; this specification claims none.

## Dependencies

- FR-372 and QSpec FR-140 own Decimal membership, on which the constructor depends. FR-384 is the native-width and host-availability precedent. FR-398 (IR-1443) is the sibling constructor and the precedent for the boundary vocabulary, the refusal names and the differential.
- CG FR-051 (merged, specified under IR-1440) is the consumer and names the carrier as prerequisite P1; CG FR-025-AC-15 defines the bindings, CG FR-028 the 16 GiB ceiling. CG's consumption of the carrier is CG's ticket IR-259 and CG's credit, not this requirement's acceptance.
- IR-1445 (text) and IR-1446 (float) are sibling carriers and not prerequisites. IR-1443 (quantity) is merged and not a prerequisite; the two Decimal constructors must not diverge in the membership rule.
- The generated oracle (CG FR-014) and the harness are CG's; the composed proof therefore runs in CG's harness, not in this repository.
