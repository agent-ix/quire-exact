# FR-403: Native Decimal operations refine the owner's metered Decimal path

## Description

Where a caller selects the native Decimal API over the carried Decimals of [FR-402](./FR-402-kani-tractable-native-decimal-carrier.md), the kernel SHALL evaluate the six Decimal operations and the four ordering comparisons with fixed-width native arithmetic, returning the typed outcome, the retained representation, the loss record and the ordered Meter charges that the owner's `evaluate_decimal` and `order_numbers` return for the same operands and target. The native API is a production facility available under ordinary builds, in the manner of [FR-384](./FR-384-admitted-native-integer-refinement.md). It is not a verification-only implementation and it does not replace the owner's arbitrary-precision path, which is unchanged.

This is the native expression that CG FR-051 (prerequisite P1) compares with the generated oracle. The owner's arithmetic, rounding and charge schedule ([FR-362](./FR-362-exact-scalar-arithmetic-and-atom-charges.md), QSpec FR-140) are the specification; this requirement adds no arithmetic, charge point or refusal for any operand the native path admits. Because the charge amounts and the loss record are where a native path most easily drifts from the owner, Behavior 5 to 8 state them in full instead of by reference. Where a quantity the owner forms does not fit the native width, the native path refuses admission with a typed capacity refusal and claims nothing (Behavior 2): it never narrows, wraps, saturates or approximates.

## Inputs

- A closed operation: `Add`, `Subtract`, `Multiply` or `Divide` over two carried Decimals (built from bindings, from an earlier result or by the total retained-pair constructor of FR-402 Behavior 3); `Negate` or `Round` over one. An ordering takes an owner `OrderingOperator` (`lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`) and two carried Decimals.
- The native Decimal type of the result (arithmetic operations only; an ordering has none) and the caller's `Meter`.

## Outputs

An admitted operation or a typed capacity refusal. For an admitted operation, the owner's `Outcome` over a native result (a carried Decimal retaining `(v x 10^T, T)`, where `T` is the type's `max_scale`, and the optional native loss record) or over a Boolean.

## Behavior

### Notation

For a retained coefficient `c` and a shift `k`: `bits(c)` is the bit length of `|c|` with a minimum of 1 (`bits(0)` is 1); `digits(c)` is the decimal digit count of `|c|` with a minimum of 1; `sbits(c, 0)` is `bits(c)`, `sbits(c, k)` for `k` above zero is `bits(c) + bits(10^k)` (a sum of two bit lengths, not the bit length of `c x 10^k`); `sdigits(c, k)` is `digits(c) + k`. For operands `(c_a, s_a)` and `(c_b, s_b)` and target scale `T`, the owner's evaluation plan is: for `Add` and `Subtract`, working scale `S = max(s_a, s_b)` with shifted operands `c_a x 10^(S - s_a)` and `c_b x 10^(S - s_b)`; for `Multiply`, the unshifted coefficients at working scale `s_a + s_b`; for `Divide`, the numerator `N = c_a x 10^max(0, T + s_b - s_a)` and the denominator `D = c_b x 10^max(0, s_a - s_b - T)`, a quotient in units of `10^-T`; for `Negate` and `Round`, the coefficient `c_a` at working scale `s_a`. Scales and exponents are summed in 64 bits, so no scale sum wraps.

### Admission

1. Before any charge, the kernel SHALL decide whether an operation is admitted from its operands and type alone. The decision SHALL use only native arithmetic and no allocation, SHALL take no `Meter` and SHALL charge nothing. A refusal SHALL be a typed capacity refusal that names the first failing quantity of Behavior 2 in the order listed there, and SHALL NOT be an arithmetic refusal, a Meter denial, a completed value or a proof result.
2. The kernel SHALL refuse admission exactly when at least one of the following holds for the owner's plan of the same operation, operands and type. In order: (a) a power-of-ten exponent above 38 is needed by a plan shift (an `Add`, `Subtract` or `Divide` shift, or an ordering alignment shift) or by the lift of the placed coefficient to `T`, whatever the coefficient (a zero coefficient included, because the charge amounts of Behavior 6 need `bits(10^k)` and the native path forms `bits(10^k)` only for `k` up to 38); (b) a shifted operand lies outside signed 128 bits; (c) the aligned sum, difference, product or negation, or the quotient parts `N` and `D`, lie outside signed 128 bits; (d) when the owner divides an intermediate by `10^e` to reach `T` (which the owner does only when `e` is above zero and at most `digits` of the intermediate), `10^e` lies outside signed 128 bits; (e) an integer that a nearest rounding mode compares, namely twice the remainder of the floor division of the reduced rounded value, lies outside signed 128 bits; (f) the retained coefficient `v x 10^T` after the lift lies outside signed 128 bits. Where the owner does not divide (`e` above the digit count, a case in which every rounding mode rounds the same proxy of one tenth of the sign), no power of ten is formed and none is a ground of refusal. The numerator and the cofactor of a loss record never exceed the reduced rounded value and the divisor of (d) or the reduced `D`, so they are no separate ground. Charge amounts are not a ground of refusal. Every operand pair and type for which none of (a) to (f) holds SHALL be admitted. The capacity region is value-dependent and is stated by this list, not by a narrower fixed interval.
3. The admission decision SHALL precede the divisor-zero test: an operation whose plan quantities do not fit is a capacity refusal even when its divisor is zero, because the divisor-zero test is an owner outcome that follows the `decimal.operands` charge (Behavior 6).

### Evaluation

4. For an admitted operation the kernel SHALL return the owner's typed outcome. The completed value's retained representation SHALL equal the owner's `(v x 10^T, T)`, not only its normalized value. `Undefined(DivisionByZero)` SHALL be returned for a divisor whose coefficient is zero, after the `decimal.operands` charge and before the `decimal.scale-expansion` charge. Under `exact` rounding, `Refused(InexactDecimal)` SHALL be returned, after the `decimal.arithmetic` charge and before any `decimal.rounding` charge or loss record, whenever the exact result is not an integer multiple of `10^-T`. `Refused(DecimalOutOfDomain)` SHALL be returned, before the `decimal.result-retain` charge, when the placed result is not a member of the result type by the owner's membership rule, including after rounding. A zero result SHALL be placed exactly and SHALL NOT be a rounding step whatever its working scale. No other refusal SHALL exist.
5. The six rounding spellings SHALL round as the owner rounds: toward zero, toward positive, toward negative, nearest even and nearest away round a rounding step, and `exact` refuses it. A rounding step SHALL record a native loss record that reads out to the owner's `DecimalLoss` under its `Eq`: the exact value as the reduced numerator, the positive cofactor coprime to ten, and the exponents of two and five of the denominator with the power of ten of the working scale kept factored (the exact value is `numerator / (cofactor x 2^twos x 5^fives)`, with the common twos and fives of the numerator and the working-scale power of ten cancelled; for `Divide` the working-scale power is that of `T` applied to the reduced quotient), the rounded retained representation `(rounded, T)` and the mode. A rounding step whose power of ten exceeds the digit count (the proxy case of Behavior 2) SHALL record the exact intermediate, not the proxy.

### Charges

6. The kernel SHALL present to the caller's actual `Meter`, in this order and with these limit kinds and amounts (each charge carrying the owner's default one work unit), the owner's charges: `decimal.operands` with `integer_bits` the maximum of `bits` over the operand coefficients, `decimal_digits` the maximum of `digits`, and `value_occurrences` the operand count (1 or 2); `decimal.scale-expansion` with `scale_expansion` the shift of the first shifted operand whose shift is above zero (left before right, numerator before denominator) or 0 when there is none, and, only when there is one, `integer_bits` `sbits(c, shift)` and `decimal_digits` `sdigits(c, shift)` of that operand; `decimal.arithmetic` with `integer_bits` and `decimal_digits` equal, for `Add` and `Subtract`, to the maximum over the two shifted operands of `sbits` and of `sdigits` plus one, for `Multiply` to the sum over the two operands of `sbits(c, 0)` and of `sdigits(c, 0)`, for `Divide` to the maximum over `N` and `D` of `sbits` and of `sdigits`, and for `Negate` and `Round` to `sbits(c, 0)` and `sdigits(c, 0)`; `decimal.rounding`, on a rounding step of a mode other than `exact` only, with the same two amounts as `decimal.arithmetic`; and `decimal.result-retain` with `scale_expansion` `k = T - p` for the placed coefficient `c` at placed scale `p`, `integer_bits` `sbits(c, k)`, `decimal_digits` `sdigits(c, k)`, `value_occurrences` 1 and one result unit.
7. For an ordering the kernel SHALL charge `ordering.operands` with `integer_bits` the maximum `bits` and `decimal_digits` the maximum `digits` of the two coefficients and `value_occurrences` 2; then `ordering.arithmetic` with `scale_expansion` the larger of the two alignment shifts (the difference to the larger scale), `integer_bits` the maximum of the two `sbits(c, shift)` and `decimal_digits` the maximum of the two `sdigits(c, shift)`; then `ordering.result-retain` with one result unit. An ordering SHALL order by mathematical value, not by retained representation or raw coefficient.
8. The charge semantics SHALL be those of the owner's `Meter`: cumulative work and results, cancellation and injected-denial precedence are the `Meter`'s. A denied charge SHALL stop the operation with the owner's incomplete record, no value and no later charge. Admission work SHALL NOT manufacture evaluator charges, and a `Meter` presented to a refused admission SHALL be unchanged.

### Shape and evidence

9. The admission, evaluation and ordering bodies on the proof path SHALL contain no loop, no iterator adapter and no allocation, SHALL NOT be selected by `cfg(kani)`, and SHALL create no `Integer`, `Decimal`, `Rational` or `Vec`. The result and the loss record SHALL be `Copy`. The read-out of a result SHALL yield an owner `DecimalResult` and MAY allocate outside the proof path.
10. The kernel SHALL carry a host differential. For every boundary operand and type of TC-951 the oracle SHALL be the owner route (`evaluate_decimal` and `order_numbers` over `Decimal::new` operands) run under a `Meter` with the same limits. Where the owner route is exercised at a large `T`, the oracle is limited to the cases the owner can materialize. The native path and the oracle SHALL agree on the typed outcome, the read-out `DecimalResult` under its `Eq` (which includes the factored loss record), the ordered charges, every counter and the denied next charge at sufficient, exact and one-less limits of each charge, with exactly one intended difference: an operation the native admission refuses, where the oracle returns whatever the owner decides, including a completed value.
11. The kernel SHALL carry, for each of `Add`, `Subtract`, `Multiply`, `Divide`, `Negate`, `Round` and the ordering, a Kani proof without a loop unwind bound that, over all symbolic carried operands and all symbolic native types within the native width, equates the admission decision, the outcome, the completed value and the loss record to an independent bounded mathematical reference that shares no code with the native path, with covers that the completed path, a rounding step, each refusal and a capacity refusal are reachable. A host test SHALL bind each reference to the owner route over the boundary set.
12. When CG's decimal row (CG FR-051, ticket IR-259) consumes the delivered operations, the memory and time of the composed proof under the 16 GiB per-request ceiling of CG FR-028 SHALL be measured and recorded under IR-1508 with the peak provenance and the terminal outcome. This requirement states no memory budget and claims no fit; a prediction is not a measurement.

## Dependencies

- IR-1444 (merged, quire-exact PR 146) delivered the carrier, the native type and the constructor of [FR-402](./FR-402-kani-tractable-native-decimal-carrier.md), on which this requirement depends. FR-362 and QSpec FR-140 own the arithmetic and the charge schedule that this requirement refines. FR-384 is the precedent for admission before charge and for the typed capacity refusal.
- CG FR-051 is the consumer (prerequisite P1); CG's consumption is CG's ticket IR-259 and CG's credit, not this requirement's acceptance. CG FR-028 owns the 16 GiB ceiling.
- The seven proofs of Behavior 11 are run only when the repository's standing instruction in CLAUDE.md on the Kani runners permits it; until then they stay unexecuted.
- The `Meter` is the owner's and unchanged; its own allocation behaviour is outside this requirement.
