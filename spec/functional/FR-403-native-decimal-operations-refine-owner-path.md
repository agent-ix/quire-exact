# FR-403: Native Decimal operations refine the owner's metered Decimal path

## Description

Where a caller selects the native Decimal API over the carried Decimals of [FR-402](./FR-402-kani-tractable-native-decimal-carrier.md), the kernel SHALL evaluate the six Decimal operations and the four ordering comparisons with fixed-width native arithmetic, returning the typed outcome, the retained representation, the loss record and the ordered Meter charges that the owner's `evaluate_decimal` and `order_numbers` return for the same operands and target. The native API is a production facility available under ordinary builds, in the manner of [FR-384](./FR-384-admitted-native-integer-refinement.md). It is not a verification-only implementation and it does not replace the owner's arbitrary-precision path, which is unchanged.

This is the native expression that CG FR-051 (prerequisite P1) compares with the generated oracle. The owner's arithmetic, rounding and charge schedule ([FR-362](./FR-362-exact-scalar-arithmetic-and-atom-charges.md), QSpec FR-140) are the specification; this requirement adds no arithmetic, charge point or refusal for any operand the native path admits. Because the charge amounts and the loss record are where a native path most easily drifts from the owner, Behaviors 13 to 29 state them in full instead of by reference. Where an integer the owner forms does not fit the native width, the native path refuses admission with a typed capacity refusal and claims nothing (Behavior 3): it never narrows, wraps, saturates or approximates.

## Inputs

- A closed operation: `Add`, `Subtract`, `Multiply` or `Divide` over two carried Decimals (built from bindings, from an earlier result or by the total retained-pair constructor of FR-402 Behavior 3); `Negate` or `Round` over one. An ordering takes an owner `OrderingOperator` (`lt`, `le`, `gt`, `ge`) and two carried Decimals.
- The native Decimal type of the result (arithmetic operations only; an ordering has none) and the caller's `Meter`.

## Outputs

An admitted operation or a typed capacity refusal. For an admitted operation, the owner's `Outcome` over a native result (a carried Decimal retaining `(v x 10^T, T)`, where `T` is the type's `max_scale`, and the optional native loss record) or over a Boolean.

## Behavior

### Notation

For a retained coefficient `c` and a shift `k`: `bits(c)` is the bit length of `|c|` with a minimum of 1 (`bits(0)` is 1); `digits(c)` is the decimal digit count of `|c|` with a minimum of 1; `sbits(c, 0)` is `bits(c)`, `sbits(c, k)` for `k` above zero is `bits(c) + bits(10^k)` (a sum of two bit lengths, not the bit length of `c x 10^k`); `sdigits(c, k)` is `digits(c) + k`. For operands `(c_a, s_a)` and `(c_b, s_b)` and target scale `T`, the owner's evaluation plan is: for `Add` and `Subtract`, working scale `S = max(s_a, s_b)` with shifted operands `c_a x 10^(S - s_a)` and `c_b x 10^(S - s_b)`; for `Multiply`, the unshifted coefficients at working scale `s_a + s_b`; for `Divide`, the numerator `N = c_a x 10^max(0, T + s_b - s_a)` and the denominator `D = c_b x 10^max(0, s_a - s_b - T)`, a quotient in units of `10^-T`; for `Negate` and `Round`, the coefficient `c_a` at working scale `s_a`. The reduced quotient `N' / D'` of a `Divide` is the owner's canonical rational: the sign moved to the numerator, so `D'` is positive, and the common factor of `N` and `D` removed. Scales and exponents are summed in 64 bits, so no scale sum wraps. The owner forms `10^k` (by `expand_one`) for every plan shift above zero and for the lift of the placed coefficient to `T`, even when the coefficient is zero.

### Admission

1. Before any charge, the kernel SHALL decide whether an operation is admitted from its operands and type alone, using only native arithmetic and no allocation, taking no `Meter` and charging nothing.
2. A refusal of admission SHALL be a typed capacity refusal that names the first failing quantity of Behavior 3 in the order listed there and is neither an arithmetic refusal, a Meter denial, a completed value nor a proof result.
3. For `Add`, `Subtract`, `Multiply`, `Divide`, `Negate` and `Round`, the kernel SHALL refuse admission exactly when at least one of the following holds for the owner's plan of the same operation, operands and type. In order: (a) the owner forms `10^k` with `k` above 38 for a plan shift (`Add`, `Subtract` or `Divide`) or for the lift of the placed coefficient to `T` (`k = T - p` for the placed scale `p`), whatever the coefficient, a zero coefficient included, because `10^k` for `k` above 38 does not fit signed 128 bits; a zero placed at a scale `p` with `T - p` at most 38 is not refused, and a zero placed at `T` has lift 0; (b) a shifted operand of `Add` or `Subtract` lies outside signed 128 bits; (c) the aligned sum, difference, product or negation, or `N` or `D`, lies outside signed 128 bits; (d) the reduced numerator `N'` or the reduced denominator `D'` of a `Divide` lies outside signed 128 bits (reachable only by moving the sign of `i128::MIN`: `N = i128::MIN` with `D = -3`, or `D = i128::MIN` with `N = 1`; `N = i128::MIN` with `D = -2` is not refused, its reduced numerator being `2^126`); (e) when the owner divides an intermediate by `10^e` to reach `T` (which it does only when `e` is above zero and at most `digits` of the intermediate), `10^e` lies outside signed 128 bits; (f) when a nearest rounding mode compares, twice the remainder of the floor division of the reduced numerator by the reduced denominator of the rounded value lies outside signed 128 bits; (g) the retained coefficient `v x 10^T` after the lift lies outside signed 128 bits. Where the owner does not divide (`e` above the digit count, a case in which every rounding mode rounds the same proxy of one tenth of the sign), no power of ten is formed and none is a ground of refusal. The numerator and the cofactor of a loss record are the reduced numerator (bounded by (d) or by (c)) and a divisor of the reduced denominator or of the power of (e), so they are no separate ground. Charge amounts are not a ground of refusal: with (a), every `bits(10^k)` of a non-ordering charge has `k` at most 38.
4. The kernel SHALL admit every operand pair and type for which none of the grounds (a) to (g) of Behavior 3 holds; the capacity region is value-dependent and is stated by that list, not by a narrower fixed interval.
5. When the divisor coefficient of a `Divide` is zero, the kernel SHALL admit the operation without applying any ground of Behavior 3, because the owner returns `Undefined(DivisionByZero)` right after the `decimal.operands` charge and forms no plan quantity.
6. The kernel SHALL admit every ordering, because the owner forms no alignment power of ten and no aligned operand for an ordering (FR-363).

### Evaluation

7. For an admitted operation the completed value's retained representation SHALL equal the owner's `(v x 10^T, T)`, not only its normalized value.
8. When the divisor coefficient is zero, the kernel SHALL return `Undefined(DivisionByZero)` after the `decimal.operands` charge and before the `decimal.scale-expansion` charge.
9. Under `exact` rounding, when the exact result is not an integer multiple of `10^-T`, the kernel SHALL return `Refused(InexactDecimal)` after the `decimal.arithmetic` charge and before any `decimal.rounding` charge or loss record.
10. When the placed result is not a member of the result type by the owner's membership rule (FR-372), including after rounding, the kernel SHALL return `Refused(DecimalOutOfDomain)` before the `decimal.result-retain` charge.
11. A zero result SHALL be placed exactly, and is not a rounding step whatever its working scale.
12. The kernel SHALL return no refusal other than those of Behaviors 8 to 10.
13. The six rounding spellings SHALL round as the owner rounds: toward zero, toward positive, toward negative, nearest even and nearest away round a rounding step, and `exact` refuses it.
14. A rounding step SHALL record a native loss record that reads out to the owner's `DecimalLoss` under its `Eq`: the exact value as the reduced numerator, the positive cofactor coprime to ten, and the exponents of two and five of the denominator, with the power of ten of the working scale kept factored (the exact value is `numerator / (cofactor x 2^twos x 5^fives)`, with the common twos and fives of the numerator and the working-scale power of ten cancelled; for `Divide` the working-scale power is that of `T` applied to the reduced quotient), the rounded retained representation `(rounded, T)` and the mode.
15. A rounding step whose power of ten exceeds the digit count (the proxy case of Behavior 3) SHALL record the exact intermediate, not the proxy.
16. The kernel SHALL reduce a `Divide` quotient by the greatest common divisor of the magnitudes of `N` and `D` by a data-independent sequence of exactly 256 binary-gcd steps with no data-dependent loop, where the state is `(u, v, g)` starting at `(|N|, |D|, 1)` and one step is: when both `u` and `v` are even and nonzero, halve both and double `g`; otherwise when `u` is even and nonzero and `v` is nonzero, halve `u`; otherwise when `v` is even and nonzero and `u` is nonzero, halve `v`; otherwise when both are odd, replace the larger by half the difference; otherwise (an operand is zero) change nothing, so that a zero numerator leaves `(0, |D|, 1)` and reduces to `0/1`. Each step that changes the state reduces the sum of the bit lengths by at least one, the sum starts at no more than 256, and the gcd is `g x (u + v)` once an operand is zero.
17. The gcd bound of Behavior 16 SHALL be covered by the `Divide` proof of Behavior 37.

### Charges

18. The kernel SHALL charge `decimal.operands` first, with `integer_bits` the maximum of `bits` over the operand coefficients, `decimal_digits` the maximum of `digits`, and `value_occurrences` the operand count (1 or 2).
19. The kernel SHALL then charge `decimal.scale-expansion` with `scale_expansion` the shift of the first shifted operand whose shift is above zero (left before right, numerator before denominator) or 0 when there is none, and, only when there is one, `integer_bits` `sbits(c, shift)` and `decimal_digits` `sdigits(c, shift)` of that operand.
20. The kernel SHALL then charge `decimal.arithmetic` with `integer_bits` and `decimal_digits` equal, for `Add` and `Subtract`, to the maximum over the two shifted operands of `sbits` and of `sdigits` plus one, for `Multiply` to the sum over the two operands of `sbits(c, 0)` and of `sdigits(c, 0)`, for `Divide` to the maximum over `N` and `D` of `sbits` and of `sdigits`, and for `Negate` and `Round` to `sbits(c, 0)` and `sdigits(c, 0)`.
21. On a rounding step of a mode other than `exact` only, the kernel SHALL charge `decimal.rounding` with the same two amounts as `decimal.arithmetic`.
22. The kernel SHALL finally charge `decimal.result-retain` with `scale_expansion` `k = T - p` for the placed coefficient `c` at placed scale `p`, `integer_bits` `sbits(c, k)`, `decimal_digits` `sdigits(c, k)`, `value_occurrences` 1 and one result unit; each charge carries the owner's default one work unit.
23. For an ordering the kernel SHALL charge `ordering.operands` with `integer_bits` the maximum `bits` and `decimal_digits` the maximum `digits` of the two coefficients and `value_occurrences` 2, then `ordering.arithmetic` with `scale_expansion` the larger of the two alignment shifts (the difference to the larger scale), `integer_bits` the maximum of the two `sbits(c, shift)` and `decimal_digits` the maximum of the two `sdigits(c, shift)`, then `ordering.result-retain` with one result unit.
24. An ordering SHALL order by mathematical value, not by retained representation or raw coefficient, without forming the alignment power of ten; the native comparison MAY use a fixed-width magnitude wider than 128 bits where the owner multiplies at equal digit counts.
25. The ordering charge amounts SHALL equal the owner's for every alignment shift up to 4294967295, where `bits(10^k)` is `floor(k x log2(10)) + 1`, formed with no loop and no allocation by multiplying `k` in unsigned 128 bits by a lower and an upper approximation of `log2(10)` of the same fixed number of fractional bits, between 67 and 94 inclusive (enough for exactness, and small enough that the product fits 128 bits), whose floors agree for every `k` from 0 to 4294967295.
26. A `Meter` presented to a refused admission SHALL be unchanged.
27. Cumulative work and results, cancellation and injected-denial precedence SHALL be the `Meter`'s.
28. A denied charge SHALL stop the operation with the owner's incomplete record, no value and no later charge.
29. Admission work SHALL NOT manufacture evaluator charges.

### Shape and evidence

30. The admission, evaluation, gcd and ordering bodies on the proof path SHALL contain no loop, iterator adapter, recursion or allocation.
31. The proof-path bodies SHALL NOT be selected by `cfg(kani)`.
32. The proof-path bodies SHALL name no owner `Integer`, `Decimal` or `Rational` type and no `Vec`.
33. The result and the loss record SHALL be `Copy`.
34. The read-out of a result SHALL yield an owner `DecimalResult` and MAY allocate outside the proof path.
35. The kernel SHALL carry a host differential whose oracle is the owner route (`evaluate_decimal` and `order_numbers` over `Decimal::new` operands) run, for every boundary operand and type of TC-951, under a `Meter` with the same limits.
36. The native path and the oracle SHALL agree on the typed outcome, the read-out `DecimalResult` under its `Eq` (which includes the factored loss record), the ordered charges, every counter and the denied next charge at sufficient, exact and one-less limits of each charge, with exactly one intended difference: an operation the native admission refuses by Behavior 3, where the oracle returns whatever the owner decides, including a completed value.
37. The kernel SHALL carry, for each of `Add`, `Subtract`, `Multiply`, `Divide`, `Negate`, `Round` and the ordering, a Kani proof without a loop unwind bound that, over all symbolic carried operands and all symbolic native types within the native width (all `u32` shifts for the ordering), equates the admission decision, the outcome, the completed value and the loss record to an independent bounded mathematical reference that shares no code with the native path, with covers that the completed path, a rounding step, each refusal and a capacity refusal are reachable.
38. A host test SHALL bind each reference of Behavior 37 to the owner route over the boundary set.
39. When CG's decimal row (CG FR-051, ticket IR-259) consumes the delivered operations, the memory and time of the composed proof under the 16 GiB per-request ceiling of CG FR-028 SHALL be measured and recorded under IR-1508 with the peak provenance and the terminal outcome; this requirement states no memory budget and claims no fit, and a prediction is not a measurement.
