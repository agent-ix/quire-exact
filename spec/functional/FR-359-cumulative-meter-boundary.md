# FR-359: Cumulative meter boundaries and request-proportional charging

## Description

When a charge would exceed a cumulative `u64` limit, the `quire-exact` meter SHALL return an `Incomplete` record for the first unavailable counter without wrapping or changing any consumed counter or admission count. The meter SHALL keep semantic-size charge traversal proportional to that charge's request count while preserving refusal order and admitted accounting. A standalone meter proof and an external consumer receipt qualify these claims as specified below.

## Behavior

`work_units` and `result_units` accumulate across admitted charges. An addition that equals its limit is admissible; an addition beyond its limit, including one whose exact amount cannot fit `u64`, is refused. A refused charge preserves the previously admitted state even when its work amount fits but its result amount does not. Semantic-size counters remain high-water values.

For each `Meter::charge`, validation and update of semantic-size requests SHALL use only traversals whose trip counts do not exceed that charge's request count. The charge path SHALL NOT traverse `LimitKind::ALL` or another collection whose length is independent of the charge's request count. A single request traversal is the intended design, but the bounded proof does not establish the number of separate request traversals. The FR-362 integer-arithmetic schedule supplies at most two requests per charge. This proof bound does not add a public request-count limit: FR-358 permits arbitrarily many repeated requests. The meter SHALL preserve field-order refusal, the first-inserted failing amount within a kind, same-kind per-admission maxima, atomicity, injection and cancellation order, `consumed()` for every kind, and the FR-358 log.

The IR-844 Kani obligation is the `cfg(kani)` module `accounting::units_proofs`, which checks production `Meter::charge_units` (the common charge path behind `Meter::charge`) against an independent field-order transition in `units_reference.rs`. Three harnesses carry it. `units_charge_matches_independent_transition` leaves all ten limits, all ten consumed counters, the admission count, zero, one or two requests of any kind and `u64` amount, and the work and result amounts symbolic, and compares complete `Incomplete` fields (`next_charge` through its inline native value), field-order refusal, the first-inserted failing amount, refusal atomicity, the admitted counters and the admission count. `units_adapter_maps_common_charge_to_units_charge` shows that a `Charge` built with the common-path setters maps onto the direct `UnitsCharge`. `units_injection_cancellation_preserves_occurrence_and_saturation` covers cancellation and injected-denial precedence, occurrence saturation and the unchanged state of a refused charge. The `Integer`-typed `Charge::work` entry and the general charge storage are host-test obligations (`units_tests.rs`). The public `evaluate_integer_arithmetic` / `Outcome::from_stop` carrier is outside this charge-level obligation and is checked by the external receipt in AC-10.

IR-844 changes private charge-amount storage to keep representable amounts in `u64` and exceptional exact integers behind a box, with two inline semantic-size request slots and unrestricted spill storage. These are implementation details, not public API or ABI commitments. Exact refusals, including negative and above-`u64` amounts, cancellation and injection precedence, every `consumed()` result, admission accounting and diagnostic logging retain the existing contract. The two inline slots introduce no public request-count limit.

Earlier Kani receipts showed this `#![no_std]` crate compiling a monolithic three-charge harness under a 16 GiB memory ceiling and 600-second timeout. That harness, `meter_integer_arithmetic_schedule_matches_reference` in `src/accounting/kani_proofs.rs`, stays excluded from compilation and is not claimed by AC-9. Neither `make ci` nor current CI runs Kani; `make kani-meter-gate` does. Trace attributes belong only on actual `#[test]` functions (including ignored tests), never on `#[kani::proof]` or production functions. AC-9 is bound by the ignored test `units_kani_proofs_verify` in `tests/meter_kani_gate.rs`, which runs the three harnesses and requires each to verify with every cover satisfied.

After the consumer re-locks to the reviewed kernel, the unchanged CG scalar tests in AC-10 provide the external IR-705/IR-17 receipt. A test passes when its own assertions hold: healthy proofs must be Kani `Verified`, while mutation controls must be Kani `Falsified` with their specified counterexamples. FR-371-AC-6 and TC-919 step 6 require the shared routed healthy, routed violating-bound, and TC-025 arithmetic-mutant controls; AC-10 additionally requires both attested receipt cases. A `MemoryExhausted`, timeout or compiler error is inconclusive for each test and does not satisfy either acceptance criterion. If the receipt fails after the Meter change, investigate the separate `Result`/`Outcome::from_stop` carrier before claiming acceptance.
