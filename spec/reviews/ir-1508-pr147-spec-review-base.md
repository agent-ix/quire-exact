## Summary

Ticket: IR-1508. Spec-only PR #147 (head 36f59e5f, branch spec/ir-1508-native-decimal-ops, on origin/main 7200065) adding FR-403 and TC-951 (native Decimal operations over the FR-402 carrier) and a one-line pointer in FR-402. Reviewed against the owner source at origin/main 7200065: src/decimal.rs (evaluate, plan, Plan::expansion, arithmetic_sizes, intermediate, in_target_units, round, ExactLossValue::scaled, split_factor_five, Coefficient::lift/retain_charge/retain, reject_zero_divisor, expand_one, sbits, sdigits, Decimal::compare, compare_shifted), src/numeric.rs (order_numbers), src/integer.rs (magnitude_bits, decimal_digits, power_product_bits); FR-402, TC-950, FR-363, FR-362; Linear IR-1508. The literal AC values and charge amounts of AC-1 to AC-6 and AC-8 and the mutant inputs were recomputed with an independent Python model of the owner path (not the crate): all literal values agree with the owner. quire validate (spec/**/*.md) reports the same two FR-390 warnings as main and no new one; quire matrix adds FR-403-AC-1..12 as untagged (planned). No cargo, no Kani, no edits to the PR. Method: quoin spec-review/base.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. The owner fidelity of the stated schedule checks out against src/decimal.rs: sbits(c, k) = bits(c) + bits(10^k) (sbits, power_of_ten_bits), sdigits = digits + k, the charge points in owner order (operands, divisor test, scale-expansion with the first positive shift left before right and numerator before denominator, arithmetic, rounding only on a non-exact rounding step with the arithmetic amounts, membership, then result-retain with lift k = T - p), the Divide plan N/D, the proxy rule (excess above digits), nearest rounding on the reduced remainder (round over the canonical Rational), and the factored loss record (numerator, cofactor, twos, fives). The ordering charges match src/numeric.rs. The status is planned, there is no memory-fit claim, no hash or pin, IR-1444 (merged, PR 146) and IR-259 are named, and Kani stays unexecuted per CLAUDE.md. On the design question: refusing a zero at an exponent above 38 is consistent with IR-1508 for the six operations, because the owner forms 10^k through expand_one, but its stated rationale contradicts Behavior 2 itself (FND-001), and it does not hold for orderings (failure-domain FND-002).

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Summary | Refs |

| FND-001 | medium | The zero-coefficient extension of ground (a) is justified in Behavior 2 by 'the charge amounts of Behavior 6 need bits(10^k) and the native path forms bits(10^k) only for k up to 38'. The same Behavior then says 'Charge amounts are not a ground of refusal'. For the six operations the behaviour itself is consistent with IR-1508 and the Description: the owner's expand_one (src/decimal.rs:1103) multiplies by Integer::power_of_ten(shift) even for a zero coefficient, both in the plan intermediate and in Coefficient::retain for the lift. So 10^k with k above 38 is an owner-formed integer that does not fit, and refusing (0, 0) + (0, 39) and the zero lift of Negate (0, 0) at T = 100 follows the ticket's single-difference rule. This only applies when the placed scale is more than 38 below T: a zero placed at T (working scale at or above T) has lift 0 and is admitted, so 'any zero result at type scale above 38' is not refused. Restate the rationale as the owner-formed power 10^k and drop or qualify the charge-amount sentence. For orderings the rationale fails; see failure-domain FND-002. | spec/functional/FR-403-native-decimal-operations-refine-owner-path.md:46 |
| FND-002 | low | Behavior 10 limits the oracle 'where the owner route is exercised at a large T' to 'the cases the owner can materialize' without defining either. TC-951 step 7 instead uses 'an exponent above 4096' and gives 'a zero result at a scale of 4294967295' as its example. A zero placed at T has lift 0 and the owner materializes nothing, and no case in the listed boundary set reaches the 4096 rule: the Multiply proxy case at scale 8589934590 forms no power, and Negate (0, 0) at T = 100 forms 10^100. Put the 4096 rule in Behavior 10, and either list the cases it excludes or drop it. | spec/functional/FR-403-native-decimal-operations-refine-owner-path.md:63 |
