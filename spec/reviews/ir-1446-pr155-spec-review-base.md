## Summary

Ticket: IR-1446. Spec-only PR #155 (head 236b0ff0) adding FR-405 (Kani-tractable native IEEE float carrier and operations) and TC-953. Read against quire-exact src/ieee.rs at head (evaluate_ieee, compare_ieee, the charge helpers charge_operand_bits / charge_width / charge_result are width-sized and natively replicable), sibling FR-402/FR-403/FR-398/FR-401, CG origin/main spec/kani/functional/FR-053 (P1-P3, AC-1, AC-3, AC-10..AC-14) and FR-028, and Linear IR-1446 (body and the 2026-11-10 host-callability comment), IR-1502. `quire validate` on both files: valid, one `ears:non-singular` warning at FR-405 line 28. No build or cargo command ran.

## Verdict

Not mergeable: two high findings. The core of the FR is sound and answers the brief. The native ops are required to be a separate implementation from the owner and from the proof's expected side (Behavior 11, AC-7). They are host-callable and not cfg(kani)-only (separation paragraph, AC-7). A host differential against the shipped owner compares bits, flags, provenance and charges at sufficient, exact and one-less limits (AC-3..AC-5, AC-8), with buildable mutants that must each fail (Behavior 10, AC-8). The fixed-count loop wording in Behavior 7 / AC-6 ('any fixed-count step sequence SHALL be stated by its count') matches the lead's ruling. The ill-typed-before-charge and width-sized charge rules match the owner. requires_bound is correctly left to IR-69. The FR-405/TC-953 numbering gap (FR-404/TC-952 reserved by sibling PR #154) is acceptable: neither id exists on main, and #154 claims FR-404/TC-952, so there is no collision. Blocking: the sqrt/fma boundary points at a ticket that does not own native sqrt/fma (FND-001), and AC-12 is a criterion only CG can execute (FND-002). The K-DR budget is undefined (criterion-strength SR) and is also blocking.
