## Summary

Structural integrity: atomicity of criteria, verification-method labels, relationship edges, TC coverage of every AC. Ticket: IR-1499, IR-1500, IR-1501. agent-ix/quire-exact PR #145 (head de34959, two commits on origin/main), spec only: FR-399/TC-947 (non-metered `Text::from_admitted`), FR-400/TC-948 (payload-byte bound P per profile), FR-401/TC-949 (loop-free proof-path normalization). Scoped to `git diff origin/main...HEAD`. Checked against src/text.rs (admit_text, prepare, check_length, TextProfile::length, TextType::admits, NormalizationForm::apply, Text, TextPayload), Cargo.toml (`unicode-normalization` =0.1.25, test-support feature), the unicode-normalization 0.1.25 crate source (public `char::` API, private tables, TinyVec buffers), CG FR-052 on quire-contract-codegen origin/main, Linear IR-1445, IR-1436 (D4) and the IR-1501 lead ruling (2026-11-11). No builds or cargo runs. K_D = K_KD = 4, 36 attaining scalars (first U+1F82), the 18-scalar NFKD of U+FDFA and a longest canonical decomposition of 4 were re-derived independently with Python unicodedata (Unicode 16.0.0, not 17.0.0). IDs FR-399..401 and TC-947..949 collide with nothing on main or in open PRs (#144 holds FR-398/TC-946). `quire validate` passes on the six files and on the full spec tree (pre-existing EARS warnings on FR-390 only). `quire matrix` lists all 17 new ACs as untagged and FR-401-AC-3 as method-without-symbol.

**PASS with low findings** (0 high, 0 medium, 2 low). Every AC has a TC home and the TC steps map 1:1 to ACs. Relationship targets resolve: FR-367 and CG FR-052 exist on main.
