## Summary

Ticket: IR-1508. Spec-only PR #147 (head 36f59e5f, branch spec/ir-1508-native-decimal-ops, on origin/main 7200065) adding FR-403 and TC-951 (native Decimal operations over the FR-402 carrier) and a one-line pointer in FR-402. Reviewed against the owner source at origin/main 7200065: src/decimal.rs (evaluate, plan, Plan::expansion, arithmetic_sizes, intermediate, in_target_units, round, ExactLossValue::scaled, split_factor_five, Coefficient::lift/retain_charge/retain, reject_zero_divisor, expand_one, sbits, sdigits, Decimal::compare, compare_shifted), src/numeric.rs (order_numbers), src/integer.rs (magnitude_bits, decimal_digits, power_product_bits); FR-402, TC-950, FR-363, FR-362; Linear IR-1508. The literal AC values and charge amounts of AC-1 to AC-6 and AC-8 and the mutant inputs were recomputed with an independent Python model of the owner path (not the crate): all literal values agree with the owner. quire validate (spec/**/*.md) reports the same two FR-390 warnings as main and no new one; quire matrix adds FR-403-AC-1..12 as untagged (planned). No cargo, no Kani, no edits to the PR. Method: quoin spec-review/failure-domain.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED. Boundaries present: i128 sum and product edges on both sides, the 10^38/10^39 shift and lift, zero under an above-38 exponent, i128::MIN negation, the doubled remainder at 2^127 (reduced and unreduced), the proxy at scale 8589934590, 10^38/10^39 placement powers, and the retained-coefficient lift. All the literal outcomes were recomputed and agree with the owner. Gaps: the sign canonicalization of a Divide quotient at i128::MIN is neither a ground nor specified (FND-001), and two refusal classes (ordering alignment, capacity before divide-by-zero) refuse inputs on which the owner forms no out-of-width integer, contrary to IR-1508's single-difference rule (FND-002, FND-003).

| FND-001 | medium | Behavior 2 lists no ground for the canonical sign of the Divide quotient, and its claim that the loss numerator and cofactor 'never exceed ... so they are no separate ground' is false at i128::MIN. The owner forms Rational::new(N, D), which moves the sign to a positive denominator. Divide of (i128::MIN, 0) by (-3, 0) in Decimal[i128::MIN, i128::MAX; 0, 0; toward-zero] passes (a) to (f): N and D fit, 2r = 4, the floor 2^127/3 fits. It must therefore be admitted, but the owner's reduced numerator, and so the loss-record numerator, is +2^127, which does not fit i128. Divide of (1, 0) by (i128::MIN, 0) needs the positive denominator 2^127 for the floor division. Add a ground ('-N or -D at i128::MIN when the divisor is negative'), or state that the native quotient and loss record use unsigned magnitudes with a sign. Add both inputs to the capacity edges. | spec/functional/FR-403-native-decimal-operations-refine-owner-path.md:46 |
| FND-002 | medium | Behavior 2(a) and (b) refuse an ordering on its alignment shift and its aligned operand. The owner never forms either: Decimal::compare and compare_shifted (src/decimal.rs:141, 445) decide on sign and then digit count over normalized values, and multiply by 10^shift only when the digit counts are equal. FR-363-AC-4 states that 'the scale-alignment power of ten is not materialized'. So AC-8's refusal of (2, 0) against (1, 38) is a capacity refusal where no owner-formed integer is out of width. That contradicts IR-1508's outcome ('a typed capacity refusal exactly when an owner-formed integer does not fit i128'), the Description's 'Where a quantity the owner forms does not fit', and Behavior 2's 'for the owner's plan' (the Notation defines no ordering plan). Either order natively the way the owner does (sign, then digits, with the multiply only at equal digit counts, which fits whenever the bound does), or state the ordering refusal as a second, separately justified native difference and update IR-1508. | spec/functional/FR-403-native-decimal-operations-refine-owner-path.md:46 |
| FND-003 | medium | Behavior 3 makes a capacity refusal precede the divisor-zero test. The owner, however, returns Undefined(DivisionByZero) right after decimal.operands (evaluate, src/decimal.rs:741) and never builds the plan. For Divide of (1, 0) by (0, 1) in Decimal[0, 10^38; 0, 38] (AC-3), the owner therefore forms no 10^39 and no N, so this native-only refusal contradicts IR-1508's 'exactly when an owner-formed integer does not fit'. The stated reason (the divisor test follows a charge) does not force the order: admission sees the operands, can admit a zero divisor before computing any plan quantity, and evaluation then returns DivisionByZero after decimal.operands exactly as the owner does. This leaves no Meter change on refusal and adds no new refusal. As written, grounds (d) to (f) are also undefined when D = 0, since there is no quotient or remainder, and the text does not say they are skipped. Prefer admitting zero-divisor operations, or justify the extra difference and state which grounds apply when D = 0. | spec/functional/FR-403-native-decimal-operations-refine-owner-path.md:47 |
