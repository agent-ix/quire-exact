## Summary

Ticket: IR-1508. Spec-only PR #147 (head 36f59e5f, branch spec/ir-1508-native-decimal-ops, on origin/main 7200065) adding FR-403 and TC-951 (native Decimal operations over the FR-402 carrier) and a one-line pointer in FR-402. Reviewed against the owner source at origin/main 7200065: src/decimal.rs (evaluate, plan, Plan::expansion, arithmetic_sizes, intermediate, in_target_units, round, ExactLossValue::scaled, split_factor_five, Coefficient::lift/retain_charge/retain, reject_zero_divisor, expand_one, sbits, sdigits, Decimal::compare, compare_shifted), src/numeric.rs (order_numbers), src/integer.rs (magnitude_bits, decimal_digits, power_product_bits); FR-402, TC-950, FR-363, FR-362; Linear IR-1508. The literal AC values and charge amounts of AC-1 to AC-6 and AC-8 and the mutant inputs were recomputed with an independent Python model of the owner path (not the crate): all literal values agree with the owner. quire validate (spec/**/*.md) reports the same two FR-390 warnings as main and no new one; quire matrix adds FR-403-AC-1..12 as untagged (planned). No cargo, no Kani, no edits to the PR. Method: quoin spec-review/integrity.

## Verdict

CHANGES REQUIRED (low only in this file). Every AC has a TC step: AC-1 in step 2, AC-2 to AC-6 and AC-8 in steps 1, 3 and 4, AC-7 in step 5, AC-9 in step 6, AC-10 in step 7, AC-11 in step 8, AC-12 in step 10. The ids FR-403 and TC-951 are free on main and in the open PRs (only #147 touches them). The FR-402 pointer line is accurate. The step counts are consistent: steps 1 to 10, with mutants run through the named steps. Gaps: admitted capacity edges, AC-9 token wording, and AC-5's extra case.
